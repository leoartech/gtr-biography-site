# Glenn Tugade Rabadon

## Biography

CAPT. GLENN TUGADE RABADON, MEAM, PhDM

A Pilot. A Leader. An Entrepreneur. A Builder. A Filipino Philanthropist.

Capt. Glenn Tugade Rabadon, known to many simply as “GTR,” is a Filipino aviation professional, Educator, Entrepreneur, Investor, and Philanthropist whose life has been defined by discipline, perseverance, leadership, and a relentless drive to build opportunities for others.<sup id="ref-1"><a href="#cite-1" aria-label="Reference 1">[1]</a></sup>

Born on November 27, 1984, and proudly from Pangasinan, GTR is now based in Manila. With more than 23 years of professional experience in aviation, he has built a career that extends far beyond the cockpit-into aviation education, management, entrepreneurship, agriculture, aquaculture, hospitality, consulting, and community development.<sup id="ref-2"><a href="#cite-2" aria-label="Reference 2">[2]</a></sup>

Yet behind the accomplishments, titles, and businesses is a man who remains grounded in what matters most:

He is a son. A brother. A father. And a friend to everyone.

And wherever life has taken him, people have given him names that speak not of his position, but of the relationships he has built.

In Indonesia, they call him “Bapak.”

In the Pacific, they call him “Brother.”

In the Gulf, they call him “My Friend.”

And to many, he is simply “Son.”

These names are not titles he earned from a position. They are names earned through trust, friendship, respect, and genuine human connection.<sup id="ref-3"><a href="#cite-3" aria-label="Reference 3">[3]</a></sup>

## 23 YEARS IN THE SKY - AND A LIFETIME OF AVIATION

For GTR, aviation is not merely a profession.

It is a way of life.

He earned his Bachelor of Science in Aviation, Major in Flying, from Air Link International Aviation School, followed by a Master of Education in Aeronautical Management from the Philippine State College of Aeronautics.

He further pursued advanced studies and completed his Doctor of Philosophy in Management at Northwest Samar State University.<sup id="ref-4"><a href="#cite-4" aria-label="Reference 4">[4]</a></sup>

Over more than two decades in the industry, GTR has served in multiple aviation capacities, including:

* Commercial Pilot
* Airline Pilot
* Flight Instructor
* Ground Instructor
* Simulator Instructor
* Crew Resource Management (CRM) Instructor

He also takes pride in being the first Filipino pilot to hold a Ground Instructor License with a CRM rating, as he describes his professional distinction.<sup id="ref-5"><a href="#cite-5" aria-label="Reference 5">[5]</a></sup>

His experience has given him a perspective that few aviation professionals possess - understanding aviation not only from the flight deck, but also from the classroom, simulator, training environment, and management table.

For GTR, every flight teaches the same fundamental lessons:

Preparation. Discipline. Responsibility. Leadership. And the courage to make the right decisions.

Those principles became the foundation of his life beyond aviation.<sup id="ref-6"><a href="#cite-6" aria-label="Reference 6">[6]</a></sup>

<span id="aviation-business"></span>

## FROM PILOT TO ENTREPRENEUR

GTR’s journey did not stop when he left the cockpit.

He took the discipline and leadership learned in aviation and transformed them into a broader entrepreneurial vision.

He is the Founder, President, and Chief Executive Officer of Sabb Aviation Group of Companies, where he continues to pursue his vision of strengthening aviation education, training, and opportunities for aspiring aviation professionals.<sup id="ref-7"><a href="#cite-7" aria-label="Reference 7">[7]</a></sup>

His entrepreneurial interests extend across multiple industries, including:

Aviation and Education

Aircraft Leasing

Management and Consulting

Agriculture and Cattle Farming

Aquaculture

Hospitality and Resort Operations

Food and Beverage

Retail and Convenience

Laundry Services

Among his ventures and investments are a cattle farm, Lapu-Lapu (Grouper) and Malaga (Spinefoot) farm, resort, restaurant and café, laundry shop, convenience store, and GTR Management and Consulting Firm.<sup id="ref-8"><a href="#cite-8" aria-label="Reference 8">[8]</a></sup>

His philosophy toward entrepreneurship is straightforward:

Do not simply build a business. Build something that creates value, creates opportunities, and creates a future.<sup id="ref-9"><a href="#cite-9" aria-label="Reference 9">[9]</a></sup>

## A FILIPINO WHO BUILDS BRIDGES

GTR’s journey has taken him beyond the Philippines, allowing him to establish friendships and professional relationships across Asia, the Pacific, the Gulf, and other international communities.

But wherever he goes, he does not measure people by nationality, position, or social status.

He believes in treating people with respect, humility, and sincerity.

That is why in different parts of the world, he has become known by different names:

Bapak. Brother. Friend. Son.

Different words.

One meaning: connection.<sup id="ref-10"><a href="#cite-10" aria-label="Reference 10">[10]</a></sup>

## FAMILY IS THE FOUNDATION

Behind every achievement is a family.

Before GTR became a pilot, CEO, educator, entrepreneur, or investor, he was-and remains-a son.

He is a brother who values loyalty, a father who understands responsibility, and a friend who believes that genuine relationships are among life’s greatest achievements.

His professional accomplishments may be visible to the public, but the values that sustain them were built much earlier - through family, sacrifice, hard work, and the determination to keep moving forward.<sup id="ref-11"><a href="#cite-11" aria-label="Reference 11">[11]</a></sup>

<span id="philanthropy"></span>

## SUCCESS WITH PURPOSE

GTR believes that success carries a responsibility.

As a philanthropist and community-oriented entrepreneur, he believes that those who are given opportunities should also create opportunities for others.

His interest in education and aviation is closely connected to this belief.

He wants to see young people discover possibilities that they may never have imagined for themselves.

Because for GTR:

The greatest achievement is not how far you have gone. It is how many people you helped move forward with you.<sup id="ref-12"><a href="#cite-12" aria-label="Reference 12">[12]</a></sup>

## A VOICE FOR PHILIPPINE AVIATION

GTR has also been a nominee of Aksyon Dapat Partylist as an aviation representative, reflecting his continued involvement and interest in the development of the Philippine aviation sector.

His professional journey has placed him in a position to understand many sides of the industry-from pilots and instructors to students, aviation schools, training, and the broader aviation community.

His continuing advocacy is rooted in a simple principle:

A stronger aviation industry begins with stronger people, stronger training, and greater opportunities.<sup id="ref-13"><a href="#cite-13" aria-label="Reference 13">[13]</a></sup>

## THE GTR MINDSET

GTR lives by a philosophy that has guided his personal and professional journey:

“Success in life is not a matter of chance, it is a matter of choice. It is not a thing to be waited for; it is a thing to be achieved.”

To him, success is not luck.

It is a decision. A decision to work when others stop. A decision to stand again after failure. A decision to take responsibility.

A decision to keep learning.

A decision to help others.

And above all, a decision to keep moving forward.<sup id="ref-14"><a href="#cite-14" aria-label="Reference 14">[14]</a></sup>

## THE MAN BEHIND THE INITIALS

GTR is more than three letters.

It represents a journey.

A young man from Pangasinan who pursued the dream of flying.

A pilot who spent more than 23 years in aviation.

An educator who continues to develop future aviation professionals.

An entrepreneur who ventured beyond one industry.

A CEO who continues to build.

A philanthropist who believes in giving back.

A father who values family.

A son who never forgets where he came from.

A brother who values loyalty.

And a friend who believes that no matter how far life takes you, you should never forget the people who walked with you along the way.

From Pangasinan to Manila.

From the Cockpit to the Classroom.

From Aviation to Entrepreneurship.

From the Philippines to the world.

His journey is still being written.

He does not wait for opportunities. He creates them.

He does not wait for success. He works for it.

He does not build only for himself. He builds for the people who come after him.

And wherever his journey takes him next, one thing remains constant:

KEEP FLYING. KEEP BUILDING. KEEP SERVING. KEEP MOVING FORWARD.

This is GTR.

Capt. Glenn Tugade Rabadon

Pilot • Educator • Entrepreneur • Leader • Philanthropist • Filipino<sup id="ref-15"><a href="#cite-15" aria-label="Reference 15">[15]</a></sup>
