CAPT. GLENN TUGADE RABADON, MEAM, PhDM  
   
A Pilot. A Leader. An Entrepreneur. A Builder. A Filipino Philanthropist.

Capt. Glenn Tugade Rabadon, known to many simply as “GTR,” is a Filipino aviation professional, Educator, Entrepreneur, Investor, and Philanthropist whose life has been defined by discipline, perseverance, leadership, and a relentless drive to build opportunities for others.

Born on November 27, 1984, and proudly from Pangasinan, GTR is now based in Manila. With more than 23 years of professional experience in aviation, he has built a career that extends far beyond the cockpit-into aviation education, management, entrepreneurship, agriculture, aquaculture, hospitality, consulting, and community development.

Yet behind the accomplishments, titles, and businesses is a man who remains grounded in what matters most:

He is a son. A brother. A father. And a friend to everyone.

And wherever life has taken him, people have given him names that speak not of his position, but of the relationships he has built.

In Indonesia, they call him “Bapak.”  
In the Pacific, they call him “Brother.”  
In the Gulf, they call him “My Friend.”  
And to many, he is simply “Son.”

These names are not titles he earned from a position. They are names earned through trust, friendship, respect, and genuine human connection.

23 YEARS IN THE SKY \- AND A LIFETIME OF AVIATION

For GTR, aviation is not merely a profession.

It is a way of life.

He earned his Bachelor of Science in Aviation, Major in Flying, from Air Link International Aviation School, followed by a Master of Education in Aeronautical Management from the Philippine State College of Aeronautics.

He further pursued advanced studies and completed his Doctor of Philosophy in Management at Northwest Samar State University.

Over more than two decades in the industry, GTR has served in multiple aviation capacities, including:

\* Commercial Pilot  
\* Airline Pilot  
\* Flight Instructor  
\* Ground Instructor  
\* Simulator Instructor  
\* Crew Resource Management (CRM) Instructor

He also takes pride in being the first Filipino pilot to hold a Ground Instructor License with a CRM rating, as he describes his professional distinction.

His experience has given him a perspective that few aviation professionals possess \- understanding aviation not only from the flight deck, but also from the classroom, simulator, training environment, and management table.

For GTR, every flight teaches the same fundamental lessons:

Preparation. Discipline. Responsibility. Leadership. And the courage to make the right decisions.

Those principles became the foundation of his life beyond aviation.

FROM PILOT TO ENTREPRENEUR

GTR’s journey did not stop when he left the cockpit.

He took the discipline and leadership learned in aviation and transformed them into a broader entrepreneurial vision.

He is the Founder, President, and Chief Executive Officer of Sabb Aviation Group of Companies, where he continues to pursue his vision of strengthening aviation education, training, and opportunities for aspiring aviation professionals.

His entrepreneurial interests extend across multiple industries, including:

Aviation and Education  
Aircraft Leasing  
Management and Consulting  
Agriculture and Cattle Farming  
Aquaculture  
Hospitality and Resort Operations  
Food and Beverage  
Retail and Convenience  
Laundry Services

Among his ventures and investments are a cattle farm, Lapu-Lapu (Grouper) and Malaga (Spinefoot) farm, resort, restaurant and café, laundry shop, convenience store, and GTR Management and Consulting Firm.

His philosophy toward entrepreneurship is straightforward:

Do not simply build a business. Build something that creates value, creates opportunities, and creates a future.
